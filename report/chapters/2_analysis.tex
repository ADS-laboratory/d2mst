The system must be fault tolerant and support eventual convergence: edges and nodes may fail or be temporarily unavailable, but the service must continue operating and recover a correct state after a finite amount of time. From the point of view of the CAP theorem, the system is designed to be partition tolerant, since network partitions and temporary disconnections are an intrinsic property of the problem. The implementation therefore prioritizes availability and convergence over immediate global consistency, while keeping the internal reconfiguration of the MST transparent to the user.
